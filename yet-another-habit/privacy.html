Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ site.time | date: "%B %d, %Y" }}

Yet Another Habit (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information or track you for advertising purposes.

The habits and related data you create in the App are stored locally on your device.

If you enable iCloud synchronization, your data may be stored in your private iCloud account and synchronized between your Apple devices. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Purchases

If you purchase a subscription or a lifetime version of the App, payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
