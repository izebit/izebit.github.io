Yet Another Habit

Some days you nail it. Some days you don’t.

Yet Another Habit is fine with both.

It shows you only what’s due today — nothing else. Check a habit off or skip it, and it leaves your list until tomorrow. No streak to protect, no wall of red squares reminding you about that one bad Tuesday in March.

Today, and only today

  • See just the habits that need you right now.
  • Mark them done or skip them — both are fine.
  • Finished habits leave the list and stay out of your way.
  • Every week starts clean, so a rough patch never follows you around.

Habits your way

  • Give each habit a name and a color.
  • Pick your days of the week, or just say “three times a week” and leave the rest to the app.

A quiet interface

Nothing on screen but the things you actually need to tap.

Yet Another Habit Plus

Plus lifts the limits and takes your habits everywhere:

  • Unlimited habits — add as many as you like.
  • Home screen widget — today’s list right where you’ll see it, nudging you without a single notification.
  • Sync — your habits on every device you own.

Yet Another Habit keeps you looking at today, instead of apologizing for yesterday.
