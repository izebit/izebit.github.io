Tres

Three fingers. One click. Any word explained.

Tres teaches your Apple's Magic Mouse a gesture it doesn’t come with: press down with three fingers on any word, anywhere on macOS, and its definition pops up instantly — no keyboard, no menus, no app to switch to.

Tres (Latin: trēs) means “three.”

— Lewis & Short Latin Dictionary

Tap the cask and install from your terminal.

brew trust izebit/tap
brew install --cask tres
